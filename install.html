Install on your phone

Android

  1. Open this page in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm. The Blanchett Exposed icon will appear on your phone.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add to create the icon.

The reader is an installable Progressive Web App (PWA). It is installed from your browser; it is not an APK or an App Store / Google Play download.
