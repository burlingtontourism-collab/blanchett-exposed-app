Install on your phone

Android

  1. Open this page in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top corner.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm. The app icon will appear on your phone.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add to create the icon.

No app-store purchase is required. Installation options can vary by browser and device.
